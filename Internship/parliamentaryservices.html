Annual Summit 2024

Global Diplomatic Forum

October 14 – 16, 2024
Palais des Nations, Geneva

About the Forum

The Global Diplomatic Forum serves as the premier international platform for parliamentary leaders, policy experts, and diplomatic envoys to address the most pressing challenges of our era. This year's focus centres on “Sustainable Governance in a Digital Age,” exploring global cooperation and innovation.

Duration & Schedule

3 Days

8:00 AM – 5:00 PM Daily

Accommodation

Preferential rates are available at the Hotel de la Paix for all registered delegates.

Transportation

Shuttle services will run between the airport and the Palais des Nations every 30 minutes.

Accreditation

Please bring a valid passport or diplomatic ID for on-site badge collection.
